⭐ Lab Highlights (2 Points)

1. Simplicity & Efficiency

Loved the simplicity of the recipe and how quick, easy, and straightforward it was to make with a minimal amount of dishes to clean up.

2. Creative Plating Win

Loved styling and shaping the cookies, and having fun creating the presentation plate with the melted chocolate drizzle and fresh banana slices.

⚠️ Lab Concerns & Tweaks (2 Points)

1. Texture & Spread Challenge

The texture of the cookies came off a little bit too chewy, and they didn't really spread in the oven, so I had to manually shape them onto the sheet.

2. Formula Adjustment

In the future, I would want to add a leavening agent like baking soda and maybe an ingredient like flour to help the cookies spread and give a softer bake.

📝 Lab Reflection

Culinary Process & Sensory Outcome:

Overall, this Food & Cultures baking lab was really fun, simple, and rewarding, and I had a great time working on the cookie shaping and final plate presentation. Using mashed ripe bananas as both the natural binder and primary sweetener made the prep effortless, though it produced a noticeably dense and chewy texture rather than a crisp or airy crumb. Creating the special presentation plate for the teacher with the warm chocolate drizzle and fanned banana slices gave it a polished culinary look. Moving forward, I would experiment with adding a pinch of baking soda and a tablespoon of flour to give the cookies better spread and a lighter, more traditional cookie crumb.
