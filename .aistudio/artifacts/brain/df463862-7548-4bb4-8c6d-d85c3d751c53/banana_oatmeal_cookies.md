📝 **Lab Reflection**:
Overall, this Food & Cultures baking lab was really fun, simple, and rewarding, and I had a great time working on the cookie shaping and final plate presentation. Using mashed ripe bananas as both the natural binder and primary sweetener made the prep effortless, though it produced a noticeably dense and chewy texture rather than a crisp or airy crumb. Creating the special presentation plate for the teacher with the warm chocolate drizzle and fanned banana slices gave it a polished culinary look. Moving forward, I would experiment with adding a pinch of baking soda and a tablespoon of flour to give the cookies better spread and a lighter, more traditional cookie crumb.

📋 **Recipe Ingredients**:
- Cooking Spray (as needed, to grease parchment paper)
- 2 medium ripe bananas (mashed smooth with a fork)
- 1 ½ cups quick oats (stirred until thick dough forms)
- ⅓ cup semi-sweet chocolate chips (folded into dough)
- Parchment Paper (cut to size without overlapping)
- Paper Bags (for group distribution)
- Garnish: ¼ - ⅓ cup melted milk chocolate chips for drizzling (or icing sugar)

📋 **Step-by-Step Lab Instructions**:
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper to the size of the pan; do not overlap.
3. Spray cooking oil lightly on parchment paper.
4. Using a fork, mash bananas in a medium mixing bowl until smooth-like.
5. Add the oats and gently stir until combined into a thick cookie batter.
6. Pour in the ⅓ cup chocolate chips until evenly mixed.
7. Scoop with two medium spoons and drop onto baking sheet. Hand-shape into even cookies for group members + one extra for the teacher.
8. Bake for 12–15 minutes until set.
9. Microwave melt the chocolate drizzle in 30-second + 10-second intervals to avoid burning.
10. Cool cookies for 10 minutes before handling.
11. Plate one presentation cookie for the teacher with chocolate drizzle and fresh banana slices.
12. Clean kitchen station and put away all dishes in the right cupboards.
